# Quantum Computing Notes

Personal archive of notes, assignments, lecture slides and notebooks from the quantum computing courses and seminars I've taken, plus a few external repos I've found useful enough to keep close.

Not a tutorial series or a polished course of my own — this is working material: PDFs, Jupyter notebooks, quiz answer keys, slide decks. Point of the repo is to have it all in one place rather than scattered across course portals that eventually go offline.

## Contents

### `quantum_computing_course2025/`
Course run by **IIT Roorkee** and **C-DAC**, supported by MeitY, Government of India. Four weekends, May 2025. Covers qubits and vector spaces through Grover's/HHL algorithms and an intro to QML. Includes weekly slides, problem sets with solutions, and three quizzes with answer keys.

### `quantum_computing_course2023/`
Earlier run of the same course series. Lecture slides, coding notebooks (basic gates, Boolean oracles, a two-bit ripple adder), and links to the full recorded video playlist.

### `quantum-computing-course2021/`
Oldest of the three. Assignments with worked solutions, quizzes, and lecture slides from day one onward.

### `QC_seminar_at_CQuERE/`
Five-day seminar notes and slides from CQuERE — general intro, quantum algorithms, quantum phase estimation, fermion–qubit transformation, and variational quantum algorithms. Includes a SWAP test notebook and exercise solutions.

### `Q/`
External material I've pulled in rather than written:

- **QML Course** — a fork of the University of Toronto's Quantum Machine Learning MOOC (edX), preserved after the original author's death. Qiskit and Forest (Rigetti) versions, plus quantum annealing on D-Wave.
- **QHack** — materials from PennyLane's QML hackathon (challenges, hackathon brief, code of conduct).
- **QuantumKatas-Solutions** — my solutions to Microsoft's Quantum Katas (basic gates, Deutsch–Jozsa, Grover's, BB84 key distribution, and more).
- **Pennylane Demos** — basics, research-oriented, and community demo notebooks.
- **AUT - QM** — Quantum Mechanics 1 & 2 coursework.
- **qosf** — QOSF mentorship entry tasks.

## Prerequisites

Linear algebra and basic Python. Some notebooks assume familiarity with Qiskit, PennyLane, or Q#, depending on which course/framework they came from.
